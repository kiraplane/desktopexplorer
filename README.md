`https://www.desktopexplorer.wiki`.

- a full Halibut → M4x1mus → Admin → Guppy walkthrough;
- direct Cave, Temple, Sol Plaza, Puzzle 9 and Puzzle 10 solutions;
- passwords, room codes and the final chess layout;
- all 23 official Steam achievements;
- demo/full-game separation, platforms, requirements and safe download links;
- WebSite, Organization, VideoGame, VideoObject, FAQPage, ItemList, Article and
